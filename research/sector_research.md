# Sector Research — Online Dating & AI-Native Relationship-Tech

## Client context
Chleo is the founder/CEO of Anaphora, a pre-seed startup (5-8 people)
building an AI-native matchmaking platform for the European market.
Chleo has AI expertise in-house at the "can call an API" level but no ML
team, and needs an external AI consulting view on what's technically
feasible, what's already proven by competitors, and what compliance
exposure looks like before raising a seed round.

## Market size & growth
- Europe's dating app market was valued at $2.18B in 2025, projected to
  reach $4.20B by 2034 (7.53% CAGR), with the over-50 segment growing
  fastest due to later-life re-entry into dating.
- Paid online dating already accounts for 60.72% of European revenue and
  is growing faster than free tiers — validates a paid-first, not
  ad-supported, model.
- The EU had 75 million single-adult households without children in 2024,
  up 16.9% since 2015 (vs. 5.8% for all households) — the addressable
  population is structurally growing, not just cyclically shifting.

## Macro driver: loneliness, not just "dating"
- An OECD report across 22 EU countries found 8% of respondents have no
  close friends at all; young people and men are most at risk.
- Dating apps see uninstall rates over 60% for non-organic users, and
  research links heavy dating-app use to higher depression/anxiety among
  users — the incumbents (Tinder, Bumble) are part of the trust problem,
  not just competitors.

## New entrants / disruption signal
Same source, same period (Q1-Q3 2025) for direct comparability: Tinder
-14%, Bumble -11%, Hinge -12%, while **PURE** (feed-based, no-swipe
design) grew +95% and hit $100M revenue in 2025. Users aren't leaving the
category — they're leaving the swipe mechanic.

## Competitive landscape
| Player | Core mechanic | Region | Relevant to Anaphora |
|---|---|---|---|
| Overtone (Hinge founder) | AI-curated matching, no swipe | US | Validates "no-swipe AI curation" demand |
| Known | Voice-AI interview intake | US | Validates voice/conversational intake |
| Sitch | Q&A intake, human-reviewed, pay-per-setup | US (3 cities) | Validates pay-per-successful-match pricing |
| Ditto | Campus-first AI chatbot matching | US | Validates cold-start-via-niche-community strategy |
| Keeper | AI + human matchmaker hybrid | US | Validates premium/marriage-intent positioning |
| Wingman | Friends write your profile (swipe-based) | US | Closest existing player to a "friend layer," but shallow — vouching only, not structured matching input |

**Gap in the market:** no identified player combines (a) AI-conversational,
scenario-based self-discovery, (b) a structured friend perspective that
feeds the actual matching signal, and (c) explainable, multi-source
matching (self vs. behavioral patterns vs. friends) in one product — and
none of the above are EU-first.
