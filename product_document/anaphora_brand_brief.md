# Anaphora — Brand Brief

## Brand essence
Anaphora is a calm, intelligent, emotionally attuned matchmaking brand for people seeking meaningful long-term relationships.

It should feel:
- Peaceful, warm, and human
- Modern and AI-native without feeling technical or cold
- Premium but approachable
- Thoughtful rather than performative
- Romantic without clichés
- European, understated, and design-conscious

The brand should clearly differentiate from swipe-era dating apps. It is not about abundance, urgency, gamification, or “winning” at dating. It is about understanding, reflection, trust, and meaningful fit.

## Core idea
**Meaningful connections, lasting stories.**

Anaphora combines:
- Conversational AI
- Self-discovery
- Trusted friend perspectives
- Explainable matchmaking

The emotional promise is:
**“This understands what I’m looking for — and helps me understand it too.”**

## Positioning
Anaphora is for people who value depth over endless choice.

Unlike traditional dating apps that ask users to browse hundreds of profiles, Anaphora first builds a richer understanding of the user and the person they are seeking, then uses that understanding to make fewer, more intentional introductions.

## Brand personality
**Warm. Intelligent. Respectful. Human.**

Anaphora should speak like a thoughtful friend with excellent judgment:
- Curious, never interrogative
- Calm, never clinical
- Insightful, never pseudo-therapeutic
- Confident, never deterministic
- Lightly playful, never flippant
- Romantic, never saccharine

## Visual direction
The visual world should feel soft, spacious, organic, and quietly premium.

Avoid:
- Bright dating-app reds and hot pinks
- Neon gradients
- Loud gamification
- Hearts as the primary visual language
- Tech-blue “AI software” aesthetics
- Overly feminine or gender-coded design
- Clinical psychology / assessment aesthetics

Prefer:
- Organic curves and intersections
- Flowing lines
- Soft gradients
- Generous negative space
- Editorial typography
- Natural, intimate photography
- Muted, sophisticated color
- Rounded but not childish UI

## Logo
The Anaphora logo combines an elegant lowercase wordmark with an abstract symbol formed by two flowing, intersecting figures.

### Meaning
The mark should suggest:
- Two people moving toward one another
- Connection without fusion
- Reciprocity
- Two individual paths creating a shared shape
- Matchmaking and mutuality

The symbol should remain abstract rather than becoming a literal heart.

### Character
- Fluid
- Balanced
- Calm
- Contemporary
- Recognizable at app-icon size
- No sharp or aggressive geometry

### Wordmark
Use a refined editorial serif in lowercase to make the brand feel intimate, sophisticated, and human rather than corporate.

Reference direction: **Playfair Display** or a similarly elegant high-contrast serif.

## Color palette

### Sage
`#2F4A3F`

Primary brand color.

Use for:
- Logo / wordmark
- Primary text
- Key navigation
- Premium brand surfaces

Meaning: grounded, trustworthy, mature, natural.

### Lavender
`#A69ACD`

Secondary accent.

Use for:
- Highlights
- Discovery states
- Soft gradients
- Selected UI elements

Meaning: introspection, softness, imagination.

### Sand
`#F2EDE6`

Primary warm background.

Use for:
- Page backgrounds
- Cards
- Calm neutral surfaces

Meaning: warmth, humanity, quietness.

### Sky
`#DDEAE6`

Supporting soft accent.

Use sparingly for:
- Secondary cards
- Information states
- Gradient combinations

Meaning: openness and calm.

### Cloud
`#FFFFFF`

Use for clarity, whitespace, cards, and contrast.

## Typography

### Display / editorial
**Playfair Display** or equivalent refined serif.

Use for:
- Logo wordmark
- Main headlines
- Emotional statements
- Match names / key moments

### UI / functional
**Inter** or equivalent clean sans serif.

Use for:
- Body copy
- Buttons
- Navigation
- Form labels
- Chat
- Product information

The serif/sans pairing expresses the central brand tension:
**emotion + intelligence.**

## Graphic language

### Soft intersecting shapes
Overlapping organic forms can represent people, perspectives, and connection.

### Flowing lines
Thin continuous lines can represent journeys, conversation, and evolving understanding.

### Calm gradients
Very subtle gradients between sage, lavender, cream, and pale blue can add an AI-native feeling without becoming visually “techy.”

### Space
Whitespace is a major brand element. Interfaces should never feel crowded.

## Photography
Photography should feel intimate, natural, and cinematic rather than like dating-profile stock photography.

Prefer:
- Natural light
- Real environments
- Quiet candid moments
- People in landscapes, cafés, homes, walks, or shared experiences
- Warm skin tones
- Imperfect, lived-in moments
- European visual cues without clichés

Avoid:
- Artificially sexy imagery
- Influencer poses
- Highly polished stock couples
- Excessive smiling-at-camera
- Swipe-profile visual conventions

## Tone of voice

### We sound like
A thoughtful friend who listens closely and notices what matters.

### We do not sound like
A therapist, compatibility scientist, dating coach, or algorithm claiming certainty.

### Example language
**Instead of:** “You are 92% compatible.”

**Use:** “There may be something worth exploring here.”

**Instead of:** “Our AI predicts your ideal partner.”

**Use:** “Anaphora is starting to understand what matters to you.”

**Instead of:** “Complete your personality assessment.”

**Use:** “Discover what makes a relationship feel like home.”

## Product copy principles
Prefer:
- “Discover”
- “Explore”
- “Tell Anaphora”
- “What matters to you”
- “Worth exploring”
- “Strong fit”
- “Something Anaphora noticed”
- “Your Relationship Blueprint”

Avoid:
- “Score”
- “Ranking”
- “Perfect match”
- “Algorithm says”
- “Personality type”
- “Diagnosed”
- “Optimization”

## Core brand messages

### Primary
**Meaningful connections, lasting stories.**

### Product proposition
**AI that listens. Friends who know you. Matches that make sense.**

### Trust proposition
**AI that explains, not just predicts.**

### Philosophy
**We don’t believe in perfect matches. We believe in meaningful fit.**

## Brand values

### Authentic
Real people, real intentions.

### Mindful
Designed for connection and well-being, not compulsive engagement.

### Intelligent
AI that helps people understand rather than simply scores them.

### Trusted
Privacy-conscious, transparent, and EU-first.

## Experience principles
1. **Slow the user down without making the experience feel heavy.**
2. **Every question should either improve matchmaking or give the user something interesting back.**
3. **AI should create clarity, not authority.**
4. **Friend input should feel intimate, safe, and playful.**
5. **Matching should feel curated rather than scarce.**
6. **The UI should communicate calm confidence rather than urgency.**

## Competitive differentiation
Anaphora should visually and emotionally contrast with Tinder, Bumble, Hinge, and other swipe-based dating products.

Where they communicate:
- choice
- speed
- entertainment
- profiles
- gamification

Anaphora communicates:
- understanding
- intentionality
- reflection
- trust
- curated connection

The closest visual territory is closer to a premium wellbeing, editorial, or relationship product than a conventional dating app.

## Brand summary
**Anaphora is quiet confidence in a category built around noise.**

It should feel like entering a thoughtful conversation rather than opening a marketplace of people.
